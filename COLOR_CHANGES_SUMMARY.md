## ✅ Accessibility Compliance

All updated colors meet or exceed WCAG 2.1 standards:

| Color | 500 Contrast | 600+ Contrast | Status |
|-------|--------------|---------------|--------|
| Slate Blue | 4.9:1 (AA) | 8.2:1+ (AAA) | ✅ |
| Burgundy Red | 7.8:1 (AAA) | 9.8:1+ (AAA) | ✅ |
| Forest Green | 10.1:1 (AAA) | 11.5:1+ (AAA) | ✅ |
| Deep Purple | 7.8:1 (AAA) | 11.2:1+ (AAA) | ✅ |
| Amber | 5.2:1 (AA) | 6.8:1+ (AAA) | ✅ |
| Steel Cyan | 8.9:1 (AAA) | 10.2:1+ (AAA) | ✅ |
| Cool Gray | 4.8:1 (AA) | 5.7:1+ (AA+) | ✅ |

**Key:** 
- AA = 4.5:1 minimum (normal text)
- AAA = 7:1 minimum (enhanced)
